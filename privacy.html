Antarman Uploader

Privacy policy

Last updated 6 October 2026

Who uses this tool

Antarman Uploader is a private tool run by its owner for their own YouTube channels. Nobody else signs in to it, and it collects no information about anyone else.

What it can access

When the owner signs in with Google, they allow the tool to:

  • upload videos to their own YouTube channels and set each video's title, description, captions, privacy and publish time;
  • read the analytics of their own channels.

What it stores, and where

The sign-in tokens Google issues, and the details of the owner's own videos, are kept only in files on the owner's own computer. The tool has no server, no website login, no cookies and no tracking. It does not sell, share or transfer any data to anyone.

YouTube API Services

This tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it, its user agrees to the YouTube Terms of Service. How Google handles data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time on the Google Account Third-party apps and services page (or security settings). Deleting the token files on the owner's computer removes the stored tokens.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at github.com/ksiva/uploader.
